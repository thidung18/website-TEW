KNOWLEDGE

BEHIND EVERY SECOND

A watch is a combination of precision, engineering, materials, and craftsmanship. Understanding what lies beneath its design allows us to appreciate not only how a watch looks, but also how it works. Every component has a role, and together they create the experience of wearing and reading time.

THE HEART OF THE WATCH

The movement is the heart of every timepiece. Mechanical movements rely on carefully engineered components working together to measure and display time, while quartz movements use electronic regulation to provide consistent accuracy. Each system represents a different approach to watchmaking, with its own characteristics, construction, and history. Learning about these movements gives us a deeper appreciation of the technology and craftsmanship inside a watch.

DETAILS THAT MATTER

From the case and crystal to the dial, hands, movement, and strap, every component has a purpose. Materials, finishing techniques, water resistance, and movement construction all contribute to the character, durability, and performance of a watch. Even details that appear purely decorative can influence the way a timepiece reflects light, feels on the wrist, and expresses its overall identity.
